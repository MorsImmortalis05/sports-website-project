# Workout planning website
> platform to plan, track, and manage your workouts. Whether you’re a 
> beginner or a fitness enthusiast, it helps you structure your training, 
> stay motivated, and see progress over time.

This project lets you create individual exercises, 
combine them into workouts, and then build weekly workout plans 
from those workouts. It makes organizing your training simple and 
flexible — you can mix and match exercises, design custom routines, 
and plan your week efficiently to stay consistent and reach your 
fitness goals.

## Installing / Getting started

Python3 must be already installed

```shell
git clone https://github.com/MorsImmortalis05/sports-website-project
cd sports-website-project
python3 -m venv venv
source venv/bin/activate
pip install -r requirements.txt
python manage.py runserver
```

## Features

* Create exercises – easily add any exercises with your own names and descriptions.
* Build workouts – combine exercises into full workouts tailored to your goals.
* Plan weekly routines – design personalized weekly plans from your selected workouts.
* Flexible and customizable – adjust order, sets, and repetitions to fit your needs.
* Track and organize – save your plans and workouts to stay consistent and achieve results.
